Area Guide

Knowing Your Search Areas

The expanded criteria now surfaces more from Adams, Weld, and El Paso County alongside the original Elbert/Park/Morgan corridor.

Elbert County — Deer Trail / Kiowa

FEATURED — WAY OF PEACE

The Way of Peace cluster sits here. Strong agricultural zoning for equine use. I-70 corridor access makes Deer Trail one of the most commutable rural options. Low taxes, buildable shortgrass prairie. 44 active listings in this search.

Adams County — Bennett / Strasburg

NEWLY PROMINENT — 22 LISTINGS

The 6-parcel Penrith Road cluster (DOM 2, $240K, ~35–38 ac) just landed here. Bennett is on I-70 about 35 miles east of Arvada — closer than Deer Trail. Flat, buildable land with good road access. Worth a close look.

Weld County — Pierce / Briggsdale

26 LISTINGS — CLOSER COMMUTE

Closer to Arvada (45–70 min). Well-established agricultural zoning; horse boarding is common in Weld. 30+ ac under $300K is achievable. Verify A vs O zoning before committing for the equine center use case.

El Paso County — Calhan / Ramah

32 LISTINGS — VALUE RANGE

El Paso County has the second-largest cluster in the search (32 listings). Some of the lowest prices in the pool are here ($105K–$120K range). Commute from Arvada is 90+ minutes. Flat to rolling terrain; good for ag builds.

Park County — Hartsel / South Park

26 LISTINGS — HIGH ALTITUDE

Cheapest acreage in the search ($100K range) but elevation 8,800–9,500 ft adds construction complexity. ~2-hour drive from Arvada. Best for off-grid buyers; equine center use case may be a stretch at altitude.

Zoning — Agricultural (A) vs Open (O)

CRITICAL FOR YOUR USE CASE

For VA equine center and boarding operations, Agricultural (A) zoning is generally required. Before any offer, confirm: (1) barndominium as primary residence, (2) equine boarding as ag use, (3) potential VA therapeutic facility designation. I can pull zoning on any parcel on request.
