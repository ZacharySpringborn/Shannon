What the market is telling us: The sub-$200K / 30+-acre vacant land segment within 75 miles of Arvada is deep — 144 active listings gives you real leverage. Much of the inventory sits in Park County (Hartsel area) and Elbert County (Deer Trail/Kiowa corridor), with smaller pockets in Morgan and Lincoln Counties. The Park County parcels tend to be lower-priced but more remote, with elevation, well/septic complexity, and variable wildfire exposure. Elbert County — where the Way of Peace cluster sits — offers easier I-70 corridor access, lower elevation, and generally more buildable terrain.

Buyer's market dynamics: Long DOM properties (like the Chaparral Valley parcels at 697 days) represent motivated sellers who have been sitting without offers. That's negotiating leverage — an aggressive offer below ask is entirely reasonable. The brand-new Stargazer Ranch listing at $150K with owner carry financing is the most time-sensitive opportunity in the cluster, given that only two of eight parcels remain available.

VA construction loan considerations: VA construction loans (VA One-Time Close or traditional two-close) require the land to qualify as a permanent site for a primary residence and typically require a licensed general contractor and construction plans before closing. Vacant land parcels must be buildable with an approved septic system and utilities plan. Josh's expertise as a mortgage broker is a significant asset here — he'll know which lenders have active VA construction programs. Electricity already available at a site (as with the Way of Peace listings) meaningfully reduces construction startup cost.

RV living on vacant land: Colorado state law generally permits RV occupancy on owned vacant land for extended periods in unincorporated county areas, though individual county regulations vary. Elbert County is generally permissive for rural land use. I'd recommend confirming with the Elbert County Planning Department before purchasing if this is a firm plan. Most rural sellers and counties treat it as standard practice during build-out.

Area Guide

Knowing Your Search Areas

A quick orientation to the major sub-markets within your search radius, to help you weigh tradeoffs as you narrow down.

Elbert County — Deer Trail / Kiowa Corridor

FEATURED AREA

The Way of Peace cluster sits here. Elbert County is a strong candidate for equine use — agricultural zoning is common and well-established for horse properties. I-70 access makes Deer Trail one of the more commutable rural options in the search. Annual taxes can be very low ($18–$1,515 range on these parcels). No mountain terrain complexity. Primarily shortgrass prairie with ravines and cottonwoods. Buildable and accessible.

Park County — Hartsel / South Park Basin

BUDGET LAND — HIGH ALTITUDE

The lowest-priced acreage in your search is concentrated here ($35K–$99K range). Elevation runs 8,800–9,500 ft — significant for construction costs, well depth, and year-round habitation. Park County has strong appeal for off-grid and hunting-oriented buyers. Longer drives to Arvada (2+ hours via US-285). Wildfire interface and WUI regulations apply in many zones. Worth a look for the right buyer, but the commute and elevation may not fit the equine center use case.

Weld / Larimer County — Northern Corridor

CLOSER TO HOME — TIGHTER SUPPLY

Northern Colorado's farming and ranch corridor. Closer to Arvada (45–70 min) and more familiar territory. However, 30+ acre parcels under $200K are rare here — most inventory at this acreage runs $300K–$600K+. Weld County has different zoning (A vs O classification) that affects what can be built and operated as a commercial equine facility. Worth monitoring for the right deal, but supply is limited in your budget range.

Lincoln / Morgan County — Eastern Plains

VALUE ACREAGE — VERY RURAL

Fort Morgan and surrounding areas offer large acreage at low prices (16300 Monnahan Rd at $110K / 40 ac is an example). Commute to Arvada is 90+ minutes. Flat terrain is ideal for barndominium construction and equine facilities. Fewer regulatory hurdles than Front Range counties. If commute is acceptable, these markets represent the best acres-per-dollar in the search. Cell service and internet can be limited.

Arvada / Jefferson County — Reference Point

YOUR HUB

The shop at 5612 Kendall Ct anchors your search radius. Arvada is well-connected to I-70 and US-36, which makes the I-70 corridor east toward Deer Trail one of the most efficient vectors for land access. Any property within 75 miles of this address is in-scope. Shannon's Windsor home at 1602 Illingworth Dr is a separate transaction — when you're ready for a CMA on that, Zachary can run it.

Zoning: Agricultural (A) vs. Open (O)

IMPORTANT FOR YOUR USE CASE

For a VA equine center and boarding operation, Agricultural (A) zoning is generally required. Open (O) zoning may restrict commercial livestock or equestrian facilities. Weld, Larimer, Elbert, and Park Counties each have distinct zoning codes. Before making an offer on any parcel, confirm the zoning allows: (1) barndominium as primary residence, (2) equine boarding as agricultural use, (3) potential VA designation as a therapeutic/service facility. Zachary can pull the zoning for any specific parcel on request.
